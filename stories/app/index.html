Open Adaptive Stories maker

Turn photos into stories a child can read with one switch. Build a story here, mark the spots that speak, and save it as a .oastory file for the free Open Adaptive Reading app for iPhone and iPad - or read it right on this page with the same scanning. Stories stay in files on your device; nothing is uploaded.

This browser is too old

Reading and writing story files needs a browser from 2023 or later (Safari 16.4, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox 113). Update the browser and reload this page.

Story

Pages

One photo makes a page. Two to four photos can share a page as a collage.

Mark the spots

Drag on the photo to make a spot: a box, a circle, or draw around something by hand. With Move, drag a spot to shift it. The numbers show the reading order.

Spots

The highlight visits spots in this order. Use the arrows to change it.

0°

Voice recordings need Safari on a Mac, iPhone, or iPad - the iPhone and iPad app plays AAC audio, which this browser can't record. Spoken text works everywhere.

Bring in a book from Monarch Reader

Monarch Reader - the new home of Tar Heel Reader - has thousands of free picture books under Creative Commons licenses. Pick one and it becomes a story: one page per picture, with the caption as a spot that reads itself aloud. This search is the only part of the page that talks to the internet.

Read

Works with a switch that types F13, F14, F15, Space, or Enter - the Open Adaptive Switch's keys and the usual switch defaults. Automatic scanning: any of those picks. Step scanning: F13 or Space moves, F14 or Enter picks, F15 goes back. Tapping a spot always works too.

Open Adaptive Stories is a free, open-source iPhone and iPad app: github.com/mikavj/open-adaptive-stories. This page is part of the project and makes the same .oastory files the app reads. Stories are built and read entirely on your device. The optional book search talks to monarchreader.com; those books carry Creative Commons licenses from their authors. Story files are for saving your own stories and moving them between your own devices; share one only when what's inside is yours to share.
